About Us

Who We Are

Kar Sewa Dal Sanstha is a dedicated social service organization committed to helping patients, poor families, children, women, elderly citizens and animals through selfless service.


Mission

To ensure support, dignity and care reaches every needy individual.


Vision

A compassionate society where no person suffers due to lack of healthcare, education or basic necessities.

Our Services

🎓 Education

🍲 40th Service - Langar Seva

No Patient Should Sleep Hungry

Kar Sewa Dal Sanstha proudly launches its 40th service, Langar Seva.


Patients and attendants can receive nutritious meals through meal coupons redeemable at selected dhabas and hospital canteens.


Our Goal: "Every patient and attendant should have access to a full meal."
